YojanaMitra: Project Defense & Cheat Sheet

Objective: To articulate the socio-economic impact, the architectural decision-making, and the exact code implementations during the project presentation to external lecturers and judges.

1. The "Why It Matters" Pitch (Socio-Economic Impact)

The Hidden Crisis (The Information Gap)

"When we look at India's welfare system, the biggest bottleneck isn't a lack of funds—it is an information gap. The government offers thousands of incredible schemes, but the majority of the population is completely unaware of them. Because of this digital divide, billions of rupees in life-changing benefits go unclaimed every year. YojanaMitra isn't just a search engine; it's a bridge to those unclaimed resources."

The Human Cost (The Micro Impact)

"Right now, there is a family struggling to survive who is unaware that they qualify for a specific government education scholarship. Because of that lack of awareness, they are forced to pull their bright child out of school and send them to do manual labor just to make ends meet. That is a tragedy caused purely by an information gap. If that family had YojanaMitra, that child would be in a classroom."

The Economic Ripple Effect (The Macro Impact)

"There are countless schemes designed to provide seed money for small businesses, agricultural subsidies, and female entrepreneurship. When citizens miss out on these opportunities, they remain trapped in poverty. By connecting the right person to the right business grant at the right time, we are giving them the opportunity to start businesses and become self-reliant. By solving this awareness gap, YojanaMitra indirectly accelerates India's overall economic growth."

3. Technology Stack & Justifications (The "Why")

Backend Framework: Python + Flask (Over Node.js or Django) Why: Python is the undisputed industry standard for AI integration. It allowed us to natively use the google.generativeai and openai SDKs without complex middleware. We chose Flask over Django because YojanaMitra relies on dynamic AI orchestration rather than standard blog-style database entries. Flask gave us absolute micro-control over our API routing without heavy bloat.
Database: SQLAlchemy Relational DB (Over MongoDB/NoSQL) Why: Government scheme eligibility is highly structured and relational (User → Profile Attributes → Schemes → Nested Conditions). Using a schema-less NoSQL database risks massive data inconsistencies (e.g., storing income as a string vs. an integer). SQLAlchemy enforces strict data types, ensuring our deterministic eligibility engine never crashes due to bad data.
Frontend: Vanilla JS & CSS (Over React.js or Angular) Why: Our target demographic includes rural citizens accessing the internet on low-end, budget smartphones. React requires downloading heavy JavaScript bundles and suffers from "hydration lag." By keeping the frontend Vanilla, our dashboard loads almost instantly on a 3G connection. The heavy lifting is done server-side by the AI.
AI Architecture: Gemini Flash + NVIDIA Mistral (Over purely GPT-4) Why: GPT-4 is incredible but too slow and expensive for batch-processing 1,000+ schemes. Gemini Flash provides a massive context window to feed entire databases into memory instantly. We added NVIDIA Mistral-Nemotron to create a Fault-Tolerant Dual-Engine. If Google's servers time out, our router seamlessly catches the failure and switches to NVIDIA. It's an enterprise-grade failover system.

4. Core Features: Code Map & Snippets

A. All Phases of Matching & Conflict Resolution (The Eligibility Engine)

File: app/engine/eligibility.py and app/engine_compat.py

What it does: The deterministic brain. It filters schemes, checks hard rules (Conflict Resolution), and assigns a confidence score.

# app/engine_compat.py def evaluate_single_scheme(self, scheme, user, use_cache=True): # Phase 1: Hard database check (Conflict Resolution) # If the user is male but the scheme is strictly female, instantly kill it. for condition in scheme.conditions: if user_value == condition.operator_logic(): return EligibilityOutput(result=INELIGIBLE, blocking_reason="Gender mismatch") # Phase 2: If no conflicts, hand over to AI for semantic probability scoring confidence_score = self.quick_score(user, scheme) return EligibilityOutput(result=POSSIBLE, confidence=confidence_score)
